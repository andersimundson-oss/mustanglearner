Flight school

A guided flight over the Black Forest, starting on the runway. Each step waits until you do what it asks. About five minutes.

  1. Takeoff: start on the runway. Hold R for full throttle, keep straight with Q/E (rudder), and at about 200 km/h pull back with S. Press G to raise the gear once airborne.
  2. Controls: W/S pitch (W pushes the nose down, S pulls it up), A/D roll, Q/E rudder, R/F throttle up and down, V flaps, G gear, B brakes. Arrow keys also work as the stick. Space or left click fires. The mouse also steers the stick (M toggles it) and the wheel sets throttle. Esc is the menu. On phones and tablets use the on-screen stick, power bar and buttons (best in landscape).
  3. Money and hangar: every fighter you shoot down pays $600, a silenced gun $300, an infantryman $15, and a cleared pair of fighters pays a $400 bonus. Spend it in the Hangar on new aircraft and on upgrades. Free flight and Training pay nothing. Your progress, cash and high scores save in this browser.
  4. Stalls: there is no warning light. Pull too hard or fly too slow and the airframe shakes, the controls go mushy, then a wing drops. Ease the stick forward and add power to recover.
  5. Landing: gear down, flaps down, about 190 km/h on approach, and touch down gently on the main wheels with the wings level. Use B to brake.
  6. Fighting: enemy Bf 109s arrive in waves, ten seconds after the last wave falls, and always ahead of you. Waves start at two fighters and gain one more every two waves. Red diamonds mark them. Flak only opens up while you fly over a battlefield. The ring is where your six guns converge at 350 m. Hits are local: lost wings roll you over, a lost tail ends pitch control, and a hit engine loses power.
